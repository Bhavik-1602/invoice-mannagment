# Product Requirements Document

## Product
Tax Invoice Management System

## Problem
Currently invoices are created manually. Need a digital system to create, save, manage, and download professional tax invoices.

## Target Users
Business owner / authorized billing staff

## Goal
A clean, simple invoice management website — NOT complex accounting/ERP software.

## Core Features
1. Authentication (Supabase Auth)
2. Dashboard with stats
3. Create invoices with multiple items
4. Customer Master with autocomplete
5. Product Master with autocomplete
6. GST calculations (CGST+SGST / IGST)
7. Save invoices to database
8. View / Edit / Duplicate / Delete invoices
9. Download professional A4 PDF
10. Print invoices
11. Company settings, bank details, terms & conditions
12. Signature and logo upload

## Out of Scope
- Inventory / Stock management
- Purchase management
- Sales CRM
- Accounting ledger
- Employee management
- Attendance
- ERP
- Warehouse management

## Success Criteria
A user should be able to:
1. Login securely
2. Create a new invoice with multiple items
3. Save it permanently
4. View old invoices
5. Edit invoices
6. Download PDF
7. Print invoice
8. Manage customers and products
